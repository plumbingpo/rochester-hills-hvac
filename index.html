The Two Repairs We Get Called For Most

Most of the calls we answer come down to two things: a house that won't cool, or a house that won't heat. Here's how we handle both.

AC Repair in Rochester Hills

Most air conditioner breakdowns trace back to one failed part: a weak capacitor, a burned contactor, a seized fan motor, or a slow refrigerant leak. We diagnose it with gauges and meters, not guesswork. Every repair comes with an upfront quote and an honest opinion on repair versus replacement.

  • Warm air coming from the vents
  • Condenser fan not spinning
  • Ice on the refrigerant lines or indoor coil
  • Water leaking near the indoor unit
See how we handle AC repairs

Furnace Repair in Rochester Hills

When the furnace clicks but never lights, lights and shuts off, or runs without warming the house, the cause is usually one component. We test the ignition, airflow, and safety controls to find it. You'll get a clear quote first, plus a safety check of the combustion and venting while we're there.

  • Furnace clicks but never lights
  • Short cycling on and off
  • Cold air from the vents in heating mode
  • Loud banging, squealing, or grinding at startup
