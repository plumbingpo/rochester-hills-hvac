Warning Signs for AC Repair

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time and money. Keep an eye out for the following clues:

  • Bubbling or hissing noises that point to a possible refrigerant leak
  • An AC that turns on and off every few minutes but never cools down the house
  • Vents blowing air that is barely cooler than the room
  • The outdoor unit humming or clicking but the fan not spinning
  • Puddles near the air handler or water stains on the ceiling beneath it
  • An iced-over evaporator coil or frozen refrigerant lines
  • A hot electrical smell or a tripped breaker whenever the air conditioner kicks on
  • Rising electric costs paired with a sticky, uncomfortable house

Warm air from the vents can mean a failed capacitor, a refrigerant leak, a frozen coil, or a duct problem, and each one comes with a very different price to fix. Done Right

Why Hire a Professional for AC Repair?

An air conditioner packs several hazards into one cabinet. The capacitor can deliver a painful shock long after the breaker is off, the condensing unit is wired for 240 volts, and the refrigerant circuit is a sealed, pressurized loop that federal rules forbid venting. Servicing it properly takes gauges, a recovery machine, and a vacuum pump, not a DIY recharge can. Diagnosis has its own traps. A frozen coil is as likely to be an airflow issue as a charge issue, and recharging a system that still leaks wastes money while moisture and acid work on the compressor.

What you get from a pro is a repair that holds. Our experienced technicians find leaks with dye and electronic detectors, base every charge decision on superheat and subcooling, test electrical parts with a meter before condemning them, and measure static pressure and inspect the blower and filter whenever airflow is in question. Parts-cannon fixes tend to bring you back in a few weeks. Ours are built to last through the heat. And if the numbers say replacement is the better call, we show you those numbers honestly, without pressure.

Maintenance

AC Repair Prevention Tips

A little routine maintenance goes a long way toward avoiding a mid-season breakdown. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Pay attention to buzzing or clicking when the AC starts, a common warning of a failing capacitor
  • Leave vents open in every room, since closing them strains the blower
  • Swap filters monthly while the AC is running hard to keep the evaporator coil from icing
  • Schedule a spring tune-up so weak parts are caught before summer arrives
  • Shade helps, but never enclose the outdoor unit; it needs free airflow on all sides
  • Rinse the outdoor condenser coil each spring and keep vegetation two feet back
  • Start each cooling season by flushing the condensate line so algae cannot block it
